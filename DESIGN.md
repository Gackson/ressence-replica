# RESSENCE TYPE 1
The physical scene is a watch face viewed straight on in soft daylight, with a neutral white surrounding field. Official watch colours take precedence over generated palette seeds.

A single responsive circular dial on an achromatic background. No visible text outside the dial. SVG geometry follows the user's 424-unit reference. Engraved-style rounded vector numerals, coplanar satellites, hairline seams and subtle convex shading. Official logo PNG is clipped to its hand symbol and used as an alpha mask, preserving the original outline.

Only transform attributes change every frame. Local wall-clock time is sampled directly. Satellite centres orbit once per hour; their scales counter-rotate equally. Seconds and hours rotate relative to the upright scales. Weekday advances continuously through seven segments. Slim uses two individually outlined weekend cells (orange on White and Night Blue, white on Red and Black). Round uses one continuous rounded arc enclosing both weekend cells: white around red interiors on Multicolour, green around silver-grey interiors on Rose Gold. Weekday cells remain white except Slim White, which uses black. Reduced motion uses one-second updates.

Dark backgrounds use a soft charcoal (OKLCH 0.235 / 0.005 / 250), with enough luminance to keep the surrounding field from appearing flat black. Background defaults to the system appearance and offers a saved four-mode cycle: light, dark, dial harmony, system. Dial harmony uses separate quiet companion colours (red / pale warm grey, black / mist grey, white / cool grey, blue / deep blue-grey, rose gold / pale taupe-grey, multicolour / neutral grey), never copies the dial fill. Text and tool icons adapt to the background.

A bottom-left toolbar uses 18px icons in 44px touch targets. It fades in and out over 500ms with no displacement on pointer proximity or keyboard focus; touch devices keep it visible. Icons match the reset control’s 0.55 resting opacity and full opacity on hover, with no tooltip or background fill. It links to Gackson’s GitHub profile, links to the personal site at http://dullblade.space/ using a simple outlined circle, and cycles the background. The dial-following icon is rotated 90 degrees clockwise. Respect reduced motion and safe-area insets.

Round hand tips are separate luminous inserts with a 1.6-unit unpainted gap before the silver body. Rose Gold uses pale inserts and a green seconds zero marker; Multicolour retains its individual tip colours. In explicit dark mode or system mode with a dark system appearance, all six dials use a near-black face, subdued rings, and blue-cyan luminous engravings and hands with a restrained halo. Dial-following backgrounds always retain the daytime dial, including Night Blue’s dark companion background. Day/night switching preserves the selected colourway, time adjustment and saved background preference. Reduced motion disables material transitions.

Model and colour captions each have small paired chevrons. Proximity to either caption reveals all four using an opacity-only 500ms fade, with reset-style half-opacity icons and full opacity on hover. Model arrows switch between Slim and Round, remembering each model’s last colour for the session; colour arrows wrap within the selected model. Clicking the dial still cycles all six colourways. Keyboard focus reveals the controls and touch layouts keep 44px targets visible.

The favicon reuses the official hand logo crop in a square SVG, preserving its proportions on a transparent background. Its ink follows the browser’s light/dark preference for tab-bar legibility.

Daytime material finishes: Slim Night Blue has fine concentric turning on the main minute disc, outer minute track and three satellite centres; the satellite annuli have radial brushing. Round Rose Gold uses a muted champagne-copper main field with continuous directional reflections, a softly rolled outer track, and warmer bronze satellite centres inside satin-silver annuli. Fine radial tool marks cover the main field and silver rings; satellite centres use fine concentric grooves. Its deterministic textures are generated once on first daytime use at bounded retina resolution and cached as SVG images below the engravings, moving with each local disc. No texture generation runs during animation. Rose Gold hands retain a solid silver fill with separate pale tips. Night Blue retains static SVG cuts and highlight edges. White and Night Blue retain the official hand mask as an applied satin-silver mark. Broad, soft reflections use darker silver midtones on White and lighter cool silver on Night Blue. A static local SVG filter derives a fine sidewall, upper-left highlight, lower-right bevel shade and tight contact shadow from the masked silhouette. Bright highlights are confined to the bevel so the white enamel never swallows the outline. Night mode suppresses surface reflections and retains the luminous hand mark.
