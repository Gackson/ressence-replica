# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Watch enthusiasts viewing a precise, functional Ressence TYPE 1 dial in a browser.

## Product Purpose
Recreate the orbital dial with live local hours, minutes, seconds and weekday. Clicking the dial cycles current official TYPE 1 colourways.

## Brand Personality
Mechanical, exact, quiet. Reference: the official TYPE 1 Slim Red and TYPE 1 Round.

## Anti-references
No straps, headings, persistent explanatory text, external decoration or invented branding. A discreet corner toolbar provides GitHub, homepage setup guidance and background preferences.

## Design Principles
- The dial is the entire interface.
- Preserve official geometry and brand assets.
- Read actual device time without accumulating drift.
- Counter-rotate satellite scales while their centres orbit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard activation, descriptive accessible time, responsive sizing and reduced-motion updates.
